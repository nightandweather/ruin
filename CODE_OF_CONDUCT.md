# RUIN community code of conduct

RUIN welcomes people with different technical backgrounds, cultures, identities, experience levels, and views about the future. A useful disagreement makes assumptions clearer; it does not make another contributor smaller.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking a person.
- Ask honest questions and explain specialized terms when they matter.
- Credit sources, prior work, and other contributors.
- Treat speculative claims as hypotheses, not status contests.
- Accept correction gracefully and make room for newcomers to participate.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate misrepresentation, doxxing, or encouraging real-world harm are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Good-faith criticism of project decisions is not a conduct violation.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing RUIN.
