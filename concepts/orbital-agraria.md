# AG-01 Orbital Agraria

AG-01 converts collector power, crew carbon dioxide, wastewater, and recovered nutrients into food, oxygen exchange, and clean transpired water. Its central metric is not maximum plant mass but edible calories and life-support output per unit of power, area, and make-up resource.

## Productivity levers

- Select compact, high-harvest-index crop portfolios rather than one universal crop.
- Match photon flux and photoperiod to crop saturation instead of adding light indefinitely.
- Maintain root-zone water, oxygen, nutrient concentration, temperature, humidity, and carbon dioxide with automated sensing.
- Recover transpired water and nutrients while preserving a monitored make-up stream; a closed loop is never assumed perfect.
- Separate crop sectors so disease quarantine does not remove the entire food supply.
- Use waste heat and carbon streams from DATACORE and the habitat when their quality and timing match biological demand.

NASA studies place the approximate order of magnitude at 20–25 m² of crops for one person's oxygen needs and about 50 m² for dietary calories. RUIN uses these figures only as anchors; crop coefficients, power intensity, and incident effects are scenario parameters.

## Executable failures

Root-zone dryout reduces productive area. A spore signature quarantines one crop group. Carbon shortage limits photosynthesis. A lighting-bus fault sheds photoperiod and power. The simulator reports food coverage, oxygen-equivalent support, water recovery, nutrient makeup, and energy per edible kilogram.
