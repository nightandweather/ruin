# THEMIS — the autonomous civilization executive

A civilization of coupled megasystems cannot be run by human judgment alone: by the time a question crosses light-lag, council deliberation, and the return trip, the situation it answers may no longer exist. THEMIS is the executive software that acts inside that gap. The module's argument is that machine authority is an engineered envelope — evidence floors, veto physics, action classes — not a personality trait, and every clause of the envelope can be inspected and stress-tested.

## Executable contract

The model couples one-way light-time and council deliberation into a full human-loop duration, compares it against the decision window (how long the situation stays valid), checks council quorum under partition, and gates machine action on an evidence score against per-class floors: reversible 40, recoverable 65, irreversible 90. An irreversible act additionally requires a veto pause long enough for a human "no" to physically arrive.

Four decision pathways result: **HUMAN LOOP** (people can answer in time — the machine defers), **AUTONOMOUS ENVELOPE** (people cannot; the machine acts inside pre-authorized bounds), **VETO-WINDOW AUTONOMY** (irreversible action executed only after a receivable veto pause), and **HOLD SAFE STATE** (nothing qualified; safety wins).

Incidents: **model drift** caps trust in the executive's own evidence; a **council partition** removes quorum; a **command cross** delivers a stale human order mid-event.

**Safety invariants.** (1) Irreversible + unproven never executes — held, not escalated to a human who cannot answer in time. (2) No authority gap: exactly one authority holds at every instant, and handback is a confirmed round trip. (3) Stale orders are quarantined and reconciled, never guessed at. A fourth, structural one: the interface offers a "sovereign" tier only so the system can be seen refusing it.

## Sourced baseline and speculation

- Light-time is physics; the numbers in the sliders are real solar-system distances.
- Deferred authority is standard deep-space practice: distant spacecraft safe themselves first and explain later (safing/fault-protection design in every outer-planet mission).
- Tier definitions, evidence floors, deliberation times, and quorum rules are RUIN governance parameters — policy choices made inspectable, not constants of nature.
