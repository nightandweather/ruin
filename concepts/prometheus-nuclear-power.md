# PROMETHEUS — civilian fission power and nuclear-electric propulsion

PROMETHEUS models the complete energy chain from reactor heat through conversion, electrical distribution, electric propulsion, industrial load, radiation separation, and waste-heat rejection. It contains no fuel geometry, enrichment, criticality, reactor-construction procedure, weapon model, or nuclear-pulse design.

The executable model exposes 40 kWe-class surface-power heritage and a speculative MW-class NEP architecture. Reactor redundancy, conversion efficiency, survival and seed-factory loads, radiator area and temperature, shielding, crew distance, electric-thruster efficiency, specific impulse, propellant, and burn duration remain coupled. Coolant, conversion, and thruster incidents fail into decay-heat safe, survival-bus, or ballistic-coast states.

NASA describes nuclear thermal and nuclear electric propulsion as complementary: NTP provides comparatively high thrust, while NEP converts fission heat into electricity and applies low thrust efficiently for long periods. NASA and DOE's surface-power work targets at least 40 kWe continuous operation for ten years. These are program-level references, not evidence that the RUIN vehicle is flight-ready.

Sources: [NASA Space Nuclear Propulsion](https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/space-nuclear-propulsion/), [NASA Fission Surface Power](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-systems-development-mission-directorate/fission-surface-power/), [NASA NEP technology maturation plan](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220017125).
