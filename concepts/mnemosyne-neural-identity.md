# MNEMOSYNE — neural identity evidence laboratory

MNEMOSYNE does not implement mind uploading. It makes the missing evidence executable. The simulator separates structural coverage, synapse resolution, fast neural dynamics, glial and metabolic state, molecular and plasticity state, memory probes, behavioral agreement, embodiment, informed consent, and post-copy divergence.

A complete wiring graph is not treated as a complete person. Coverage is deliberately computed across five independent biological layers, while activation requires dynamic-state, memory, behavior, embodiment, and consent gates. Destructive electron-microscopy capture can improve structural evidence but cannot establish experiential continuity. Multiple instances enter **FORKED PERSONHOOD**: each receives independent authority, and no copy may delete another.

Executable incidents quarantine corrupted state, isolate an unstable sensory loop, and split authority after a fork. The module is a research and ethics simulator, not medical software, a diagnostic device, or evidence that consciousness can be transferred.

## Research baseline

- A 2025 brain-to-voice neuroprosthesis decoded a constrained speech-related signal from 256 implanted microelectrodes; it did not read a whole mind.
- The 2024 adult fruit-fly connectome contains roughly 140,000 neurons and more than 50 million synapses, currently the frontier for a complete adult-animal brain wiring map.
- MICrONS linked structure and function in one cubic millimetre of mouse visual cortex, demonstrating both the value and enormous scale of mammalian functional connectomics.

Sources: [Nature brain-to-voice neuroprosthesis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09127-3), [Nature adult fly wiring diagram](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y), [Nature MICrONS project](https://www.nature.com/immersive/d42859-025-00001-w/index.html).
