# VERITAS — when a validated model stops describing the world

Every other module in RUIN asks whether a machine will hold. VERITAS asks whether the model of that machine still describes it. A validated model does not fail loudly: the world drifts, the equations do not, and the certificate on the wall keeps saying the same thing.

## Executable contract

One model is audited over decades. Real error accumulates at a rate set by how much of the model is sourced physics rather than invention, multiplied by how far from its validated envelope it is being run. Recalibration reduces the error it can see — never the error the observation programme is too thin to resolve. Detected error passes through one more filter before anyone reads it: the share of anomalies written off as instrument error.

The quantity the module exists to expose is the **blind gap** — true error minus reported error — and the **silent window**, the years between the model becoming wrong enough to invalidate decisions and anyone being able to say so. A model can be past its action limit for a decade while its residuals sit quietly below the alarm threshold and its certificate remains current.

The audit runs against this repository's own portfolio, rated by the sourced fraction its engineering notes declare. HELIOS thermal derating and the ODYSSEY link budget survive the audit. KESSLER's encounter coefficients, MNEMOSYNE's identity thresholds, and the IGNIS fusion branch do not — the least grounded models open the longest silent windows, which is the result the module was built to be able to produce about itself.

Incidents: **regime shift** (the world leaves the envelope; the model stays put), **sensor bias** (systematic bias silences the residuals, not the error), and **validation lapse** (the calibration programme stops being funded).

**Safety invariant — certification is scope, not reputation.** A model validated more than five years ago, or run outside the envelope it was validated in, is refused irreversible-action authority. It may still advise. Nothing in this module renews a certificate quietly, because the failure it exists to expose is precisely a certificate that outlived its evidence.

## Sourced baseline and speculation

- Model drift, and the distinction between interpolating inside a validated envelope and extrapolating beyond it, are standard verification-and-validation concepts.
- Systematic sensor bias defeats residual monitoring because the residuals themselves go quiet, and anomalies dismissed as instrument error are a recognised route to silent failure.
- The drift rates, grounded fractions, detection curve, and calibration efficacy are RUIN scenario parameters — this module's own numbers are exactly the kind of number it exists to distrust.
