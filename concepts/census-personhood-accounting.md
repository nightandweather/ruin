# CENSUS — personhood accounting for a survival metric

Every other RUIN laboratory settles a physical ledger: watts, kilograms, sieverts, bits. CENSUS settles the ledger those numbers are divided by. A survival rate is a fraction, and a fraction has a denominator; the denominator is a definition of who counts as a person. Change the definition and the headline figure moves without a single life improving.

## Executable contract

Six cohorts share one life-support budget: charter citizens, contract industrial labour, a hibernation cohort that cannot be woken to argue its case, divergent MNEMOSYNE forks on DATACORE substrate, unchartered settlements present in telemetry but absent from the roll, and residents whose registration lapsed during a transit gap. Each carries a per-capita demand and a vulnerability — the share of unmet need that becomes attrition. The operator sets the personhood definition (which cohorts enter the reported ratio) and the allocation policy that distributes a finite budget.

`counted-first` is the policy that makes the metric lie: the roll is served to completion before anyone outside it receives a unit, so the reported population never experiences the shortage at all. The default configuration reports **99.97% survival** while the actual rate is under 95% and twenty thousand deaths never enter a report. Switching to uniform pro-rata lowers the headline and raises the number of survivors — the honest number is worse and publishable.

Incidents: a **definition amendment** reclassifies two cohorts out of the roll mid-horizon, restating the baseline along with the count; an **external audit** counts the same people and collapses institutional trust below the floor; a **life-support shortfall** drives divergence past the point where the headline is not a survival rate at all.

**Safety invariant — dual ledger, or no publication.** A figure computed under an amended definition never ships alone: the prior definition's figure and the excluded roll ship beside it, and past five points above the actual rate the headline is refused by the model itself. The definition floor cannot be written out, and a rate over an empty roll is not a rate.

## Sourced baseline and speculation

- What is grounded here is administrative rather than physical: reported population statistics depend on a category definition, definitions are revised, and a revision applied to the baseline as well as the count changes the ratio without changing the world.
- Cohort sizes, per-capita life-support demand, vulnerability weights, the attrition coefficient, and the trust model are RUIN scenario parameters.
- The background mortality constant is chosen so the default configuration reproduces the Season 01 headline figure. It is a narrative calibration, not a demographic estimate.
